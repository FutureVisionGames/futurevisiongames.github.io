# Future Vision Games

Indie games with vision · play free in your browser.

Live site: https://futurevisiongames.github.io

- [Candle Rush](https://futurevisiongames.github.io/games/candle-rush/): Surf real stock-market charts. Hold for long, let go for short. Don't get liquidated.
- [Meme Pump](https://futurevisiongames.github.io/games/meme-pump/): Ape into fresh meme coins and sell before the rug. Or launch your own… and rug it?
- [Shakeout](https://futurevisiongames.github.io/games/shakeout/): You are the whale. Hunt the stops, spring the trap, pump it, dump it.
- [Stop Hunt](https://futurevisiongames.github.io/games/stop-hunt/): Pass a prop-firm trading challenge on real NQ futures data. One breach and the run is over.
- [Orderflow Wizard](https://futurevisiongames.github.io/games/orderflow-wizard/): Learn to read the heatmap, footprint, DOM and tape on real NQ price action. Bounce or break?
- [Pattern Sniper](https://futurevisiongames.github.io/games/pattern-sniper/): A trading reaction-time warm-up: spot the candle pattern, call long or short, beat the clock.
- [Glowball Politics](https://futurevisiongames.github.io/games/glowball-politics/): A satirical nuclear world-politics war game where diplomacy fails and the cows fly.
- [Reef Tank](https://futurevisiongames.github.io/games/reef-tank/): An interactive 3D coral reef aquarium. Stir the water, feed the fish, unwind.
- [Night Reef](https://futurevisiongames.github.io/games/night-reef/): An interactive 3D coral reef after dark. Sweep your dive torch and feed the night fish.
