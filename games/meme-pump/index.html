About the game

A fast, satirical game about meme coin launchpads, with two ways to play. In Degen mode, coins launch every few seconds and each one secretly has a fate: a rug, a dev dump, a honeypot you can never sell… or the rare runner that does 100×. Read the red flags, ape in, sell before the dev pulls the plug.

In Dev mode you launch your own coin and survive a 7-day launch week. Shill, pay KOLs, run meme contests, handle events, and watch hype, trust and heat. Build a cult… or rug it and hope the on-chain sleuth doesn't find you.

Features

  • Six hidden coin fates, real bonding-curve math
  • Red flags: dev wallet, serial devs, unlocked liquidity, bundled wallets
  • XP unlocks tools: Honeypot Scanner, Holder Map, MEV Bot…
  • Dev mode with 11 actions, 11 events and many endings
  • Turn 2 SOL into 1,000

CONTROLS

Mouse or touch. Degen keys: 1–4 buy, Q / W / E sell, ↑ ↓ switch coins, Esc pause.

BEST ON

Desktop · Phone. Progress is saved in your browser.

PLEASE NOTE

Satire. Every coin, person and platform is fictional, and all money is play money. Rug pulls are real scams and illegal.
