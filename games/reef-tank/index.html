About the game

A living 3D coral reef in your browser. Schools of fish drift through sunlit water, corals sway and light ripples across the sand.

There is no score and no timer. Stir the water with your mouse, drop some food and watch the reef come to you. Put it on full screen and leave it running.

Features

  • Real-time 3D reef with schooling fish
  • Interactive water you can stir with the mouse
  • Click to feed the fish
  • Full-screen mode for a living-room aquarium
  • Runs entirely in the browser

CONTROLS

Move the mouse to stir the water · Click to drop food · F for full screen

BEST ON

Desktop. Progress is saved in your browser.
