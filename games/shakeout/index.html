About the game

Shakeout puts you in the market maker's chair. With $5,000,000 and a fictional coin called ORCA, you run the classic playbook: accumulate quietly, hunt the stops under support, squeeze the shorts, mark it up and distribute at the top. The whole Wyckoff cycle, from the other side.

The crowd is simulated trader by trader, with stops, leverage and liquidation prices, all visible on a liquidity map. Be too obvious and the heat meter fills: at 100% the exchange freezes your account.

Features

  • 15 tools: icebergs, stop hunts, flushes, squeezes, fake walls, hype & FUD
  • 7 missions through the Wyckoff cycle, plus a sandbox
  • Wyckoff labels print as you create them: SPRING, UPTHRUST, SOS…
  • The Manipulator's Handbook: spot each tactic on real charts
  • 7 ranks, from Plankton to The Composite Man

CONTROLS

Click the tool buttons or use the keys on them. Space pauses, 1–3 change speed.

BEST ON

Desktop (keyboard). Progress is saved in your browser.

PLEASE NOTE

ORCA is fictional and all money is simulated. Market manipulation is illegal in real markets; the point is to recognise it.
